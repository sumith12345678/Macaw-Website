Press Quality Standards

Artwork Guidelines for Perfect Output

Ensure your files are calibrated for press-perfection with zero color-shift.

CMYK COLORS

The artwork file saved in CMYK colors is the correct standard format, like when printing on high-grade stock. Color is formulated through the precision combination of 4 inks: Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, and Key (Black). Keep in mind that printed shades reflect natural light for genuine color depth.

NO RGB COLORS

The RGB color gamut is essentially engineered for luminous computer monitors, TV screens, and smartphones. RGB files submitted for physical printing will undergo automatic gamut conversion to CMYK. Always design or convert your vector profiles to CMYK before submission.

PANTONE SUPPORT

We formulate genuine Pantone Matching System (PMS) spot inks. If your brand guidelines mandate strict corporate spot colors, specify your Pantone code when placing the order so our press technicians can mix custom formulated spot inks for zero-variance consistency.

PDF FILE FORMAT

PDF/X-1a or vector PDF artwork files are highly recommended. Ensure that all typography has been converted to outlines/curves (Ctrl+Shift+O), all raster imagery is embedded at 300 DPI or higher, and artwork includes 3mm bleed margin.
